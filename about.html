Our Story

PhillySports.com has been in the making since 2006 — and we're just getting started.

We're building a community for passionate Philly sports fans who want more than hot takes and daily blog posts. This is a place to interact, compete, and actually have a say in what gets built next.

What We're About

We're not trying to be another talking head — we're building something our members own a piece of. The goal is simple: make being a Philly sports fan more fun and more rewarding.

  • Trivia competitions and tournaments
  • Curated content from creators we respect
  • Exclusive events and members-only experiences
  • Free @phillysports.com email addresses
  • Leveraging our community size for deals on tickets, merch, and collectibles
